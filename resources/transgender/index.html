Transgender Health & Research

Identity, medicine, outcomes, and the freedom to ask better questions.

Transgender and gender-diverse lives are not reducible to diagnoses or medical interventions. Some people pursue hormones or surgery; others do not. This page treats identities with respect while collecting clinical guidance and contemporary studies of care and outcomes.

Research findings have different strengths. A clinical guideline summarizes professional recommendations; a systematic review synthesizes multiple studies; an observational cohort can show associations without proving causation. Those distinctions matter.
