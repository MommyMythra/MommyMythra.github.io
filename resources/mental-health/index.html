Mental Health Resources

Finding someone to talk to, practical care, and support that meets you where you are.

Seeking support can mean many different things: needing someone to listen, finding a therapist, asking how insurance or cost works, looking for affirming care, or getting help during a crisis. These are different needs and deserve different resources.

The services below are primarily for the United States unless otherwise noted. Hours and availability can change; check each service directly. No account, diagnosis, or explanation is required to read this page.

Immediate support

United States · For crisis support or urgent distress.

24/7 · US · Call or text 988

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Free, confidential crisis support by phone, text, or online chat. If an emergency poses immediate danger, local emergency services may be appropriate.

Read source ↗

24/7 · US · LGBTQ+ young people

The Trevor Project

Free call, text, and chat crisis counseling focused on LGBTQ+ young people. Its services are intended for youth, generally under age 25.

Read source ↗

Peer support and information

You do not have to be in a crisis to want company or answers.

US and Canada · Limited operating hours

Trans Lifeline — Peer Support Hotline

Peer-led conversation for trans and questioning people. Not a 24/7 crisis service; verify current hours on the official page. US: (877) 565-8860.

Read source ↗

US · Non-crisis information and navigation

NAMI — HelpLine and Resource Directory

Mental-health information, emotional support, and help finding local organizations. Not an emergency hotline; hours are posted on site.

Read source ↗

Finding ongoing care

Directories and practical next steps.

US · Treatment directory

SAMHSA — FindTreatment.gov

Search nearby mental-health and substance-use treatment providers; filters include location and type of service.

Read source ↗

US · Public-health resource guide

SAMHSA — Find Help and Support

Government portal connecting visitors to crisis, treatment, and general mental-health support resources.
