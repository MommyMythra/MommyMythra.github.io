THE RESOURCE WING

Knowledge with the lights on.

For the questions that deserve better than rumor, fear, or silence.

A library of paths out into the world: clinical references, open research, plain-language explanations, and places where you can ask for help. These topics are part of my life, but this wing is for anyone who arrives with questions.

This is a curated starting point, not an attempt to diagnose visitors, prescribe treatments, or win arguments by overwhelming anyone with citations. Where the evidence is limited or contested, I want to say so. Where someone needs support, I want it to be easy to find.
